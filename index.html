How a Prancer is born

One hash. Three layers.

Nothing is picked by hand. The mint transaction's hash decides everything, and the whole generator lives on chain — no servers, no files that can go missing.

01

Genes

Coats follow real feline colour genetics — the orange gene, dilute, tabby patterns, white spotting, colorpoint — rendered in neon or lit. Rarity has a reason: a male calico is about 1 in 3,000, here as in life.

02

Temperament

A hidden personality profile (the Feline Five) sets how the cat carries itself — ears, tail, eyes, whiskers — mapped from published cat body-language research. Once a loop it does its signature thing. Tap it and it reacts in character.

Slow blinkDouble-takeYawnSide-eye
03

Theme song

Tap any cat and it sings. The purr is the bass, its paws are the drums, and its meows carry a melody in its own key — rising if it's content, falling if it isn't. Style comes from temperament; the room shapes the reverb.

What do I get?

A one-of-a-kind cat as a Tezos token: a still cover image for marketplaces, and the live piece on Aleatory — walking, reacting and singing.

Why can't I hear it right away?

Browsers don't allow sound to autoplay. Tap or click the cat once and its theme song starts; press M to mute.

What can I do with my cat?

Tap it: it reacts in character and sings its theme song. Two buttons appear bottom left: 🔊 starts, mutes and unmutes the song, and i opens its card (name, title, song and every trait). On a keyboard, M and I do the same.

Can I choose my cat?

No — and that's the fun. The previews here are possibilities; your cat is born from your mint.

Can I share my cat as a GIF?

Yes: paste its link or number into Make a GIF above. Your cat is redrawn walking one full loop, ready to download and post.

Where can I see my cat's traits?

On the live piece, tap the i button (bottom left) or press I for its card: name, title, song and every trait. For rarity, look it up here with your mint's operation hash.

Where does the art live?

Entirely on the Tezos chain: the generator is a single self-contained file of about 31 KB (compressed) with no outside assets.
