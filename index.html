    Good to know

    Questions.

    Why can't I hear it right away?

    Browsers don't let sound autoplay. Tap any cat once and the music starts.

    Is connecting my wallet safe?

    Yes. Connecting is read-only: we only ask your wallet for your address. No transactions, no signatures. You can also just paste an address or .tez name.

    Where does the art live?

    Right on the Tezos chain. Each generator is a single self-contained file, and your cat is drawn live from its mint, every time.

    Can I choose my cat?

    Nope, and that's the fun of it. Your cat is born from your own mint. The cats you meet on this page are just possibilities.
