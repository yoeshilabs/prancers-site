PRANCERS

Cats that walk on the beat. Every Prancer is born from its mint hash, with a coat from real feline genetics, a room of its own, a temperament you can read in its body language, a name, and a theme song made of purrs, paws and meows.

Edition256cats, each one of a kind
Mint price10 ꜩon Aleatory
LaunchOct 1live the moment Aleatory mainnet opens
StorageOn-chainone self-contained file on Tezos; no servers, no outside files

Every Prancer comes with

  • A looping walkAn animated cat walking on the beat of its own song.
  • A temperamentBody language, a signature move each loop, and a reaction when you tap it.
  • A theme songPurr bass, paw drums and a meow melody, in its own key and style.
  • A roomWalls, paint, decor, art and a rug, all generated.
  • A namePart of the token's name on chain.
  • Its own cardTap the i button (or press I) to see every trait. 🔊 starts and mutes the song.

On-chain names

Each token is named on chain from its cat's generated name, so marketplaces show the name, not just a number.

Prancers #12 · Mochi Purrington

First name

About 130 pet names (Biscuit, Mochi, Pixel, Bowie), names that suit the coat (Marmalade for gingers, Snowball for white cats, Lucky for gold), or made-up ones like Mimi and Kobean.

Honorific

Sometimes a title in front: Sir, Lady, Captain, Professor, Duchess, Lil'. Cats wearing a crown are King or Queen; chonky cats may be Big.

Family name

Most cats have one: Purrington, Whiskerton, Toebeanbottom, McWhiskers, von Floof. Built from 20 cat words and 14 endings, plus Mc and von forms.

  • Clover Tabbyley
  • Captain Popo Pounceski
  • Tata Toebeanbottom

8,340 different names in 10,000 simulated mintsabout 4 pairs of cats sharing a name across all 256

Each cat also has a title, like “Clover Prances at 128 BPM” or “Bean Pads Through the Posey Paws Gallery”, shown on its card.
